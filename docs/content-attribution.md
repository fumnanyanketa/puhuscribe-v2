# Content Attribution

> **Status (2026-06-07): content is being migrated to genuine CC sources.**
> This file describes what is *actually* the provenance of the content, not an
> aspiration. It is kept honest on purpose — an earlier version attached CC
> attribution to content that was in fact LLM-generated, which is the root cause
> of the content-quality problems tracked in
> `docs/CONTENT-CORRECTIONS-HANDOFF.md`.

## Current state of the seeded content (must be replaced)

The vocabulary (`supabase/seeds/01_vocabulary.sql`), sentence pairs
(`02_sentences.sql`), and mnemonics (`03_sprint_mnemonics.sql`) currently in the
repository and the live database were **generated by Claude (Anthropic)** in
early sessions. They were **not** ingested from the Creative Commons corpora
named below, despite previously carrying that attribution. They contain known
errors (wrong pronunciations, a few wrong translations and spellings) and are
**pending replacement**. Do not represent this content as CC-sourced.

## How content becomes genuinely CC-sourced

`scripts/ingest/` is a reproducible pipeline that regenerates the seeds from the
real corpora below. Once its output replaces the generated seeds, the
attribution in this file applies in full. Until then, treat the live content as
generated and under correction.

| Layer | Source | License |
|---|---|---|
| Vocabulary frequency rank + ordering | Leipzig Corpora Collection — Finnish, https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/en/download/Finnish | CC BY 4.0 |
| English glosses + IPA pronunciation + part of speech | kaikki.org Wiktextract (English Wiktionary), https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Finnish/ | CC BY-SA |
| Sentence pairs (Finnish + English) | Tatoeba Project, https://tatoeba.org | CC BY 2.0 FR |
| Audio (native-speaker voices, where available) | Tatoeba audio export | CC BY 2.0 FR |

Required attribution strings once loaded:
- Leipzig: "Frequency ordering derived from the Leipzig Corpora Collection (Finnish), CC BY 4.0."
- Wiktionary/kaikki: "Definitions and IPA from English Wiktionary via kaikki.org, CC BY-SA 4.0."
- Tatoeba: "Sentences from the Tatoeba Project (tatoeba.org), CC BY 2.0 FR."

## puhekieli (spoken Finnish)

There is no Creative Commons parallel kirjakieli↔puhekieli corpus. Puhekieli is
produced by a conservative rule-based transform of real Tatoeba kirjakieli and
**must be verified by a Finnish speaker before it is shipped** (see
`scripts/ingest/README.md` → "The honest gap"). It is original derived content,
not sourced from any restricted corpus.

## Mnemonics

Mnemonics have no CC source and are deferred (handoff §0). Any mnemonics shipped
in future will be generated and then verified by a Finnish speaker before going
live; they will be labelled as original generated content, never as CC-sourced.

## Excluded source

The Kotus "Frequency Lexicon of Finnish Newspaper Language" (CC BY-ND-NC) was
reviewed during research but is **excluded**: its NonCommercial and
NoDerivatives terms are incompatible with a commercial application. Leipzig
(CC BY 4.0) is the only frequency source used.
