# Content Attribution

## Vocabulary Frequency Ordering
Vocabulary frequency ordering is derived from the Leipzig Corpora Collection Finnish corpus, licensed CC BY 4.0. Source: https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/en/download/Finnish. The Leipzig corpus is a large text collection built from web-crawled Finnish text; frequency ranks in this codebase are approximations based on that corpus ordering.

## Seed Sentences
Some seed sentences are adapted from the Tatoeba Project (tatoeba.org), licensed CC BY 2.0 FR. Attribution: "Some sentences sourced from the Tatoeba Project (tatoeba.org), CC BY 2.0 FR." Adapted sentences may have been modified for level-appropriateness, accuracy, or dual-register (kirjakieli/puhekieli) pairing.

## Claude-Generated Content
Remaining vocabulary entries, sentence pairs, mnemonics, and cultural notes are generated by Claude (Anthropic) and verified against uusikielemme.fi grammar documentation and Wiktionary Finnish entries. This content is original and not derived from any restricted corpus.

## Excluded Source
The Kotus "Frequency Lexicon of Finnish Newspaper Language" (CC BY-ND-NC) was reviewed during research but is excluded from this codebase. Its NonCommercial and NoDerivatives license terms are incompatible with a commercial application.
