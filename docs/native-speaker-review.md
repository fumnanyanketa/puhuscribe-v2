# PuhuScribe — Finnish review (Starter Pack, 104 sentences)

**For a Finnish native speaker — thank you for your time.**

These are the 104 everyday sentences every beginner drills first in the app (the "Starter pack"). You do **not** need to open the app to do this — just read the list.

- **Kirjakieli** = standard *written* Finnish. Already machine-checked (Voikko, the engine behind LibreOffice's Finnish spell-check), so this should be clean — but flag anything stiff or unnatural.
- **Puhekieli** = the everyday *spoken* (Helsinki) form. This has **NOT** been machine-checked — **this column is what most needs your eyes.** Is it how people actually say it? Too formal? Wrong dialect? Just say so.
- **English** = the intended meaning.

How to mark it up: in the last column, write **OK** if it's fine, or write the better Finnish if not. Anything you're unsure about, flag it. (Single words in the app — and their pronunciations — are validated separately against Wiktionary, so they're not in this list; this is the sentence + spoken-form pass.)
