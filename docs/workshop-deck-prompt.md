## PROMPT (paste into Claude Design)

Design a **5-slide presentation deck** for a **5-minute, demo-led talk** introducing
a Finnish-learning app called **PuhuScribe** to a small room of adult immigrants in
Finland. The single goal of the talk is to get people to **install the app and become
testers**. Keep every slide minimal — one idea, big type, lots of breathing room. This
is a live, demo-led talk, so slides are backdrops, not documents. **16:9.**

**Brand & visual system (match the real app exactly):**
- Background: a soft "snow" white — vertical gradient from `#FFFFFF` to `#F1F0F6`.
- Ink (text): near-black `#1B1A20`; secondary text `#56525E`.
- Signature element: a glossy **gradient orb** (a circle) using stops
  `#4FB0C8 → #2F86C2 → #6B4FC6 → #383583` (teal → blue → violet → deep indigo). Use it
  as the hero mark on the title slide and as a small recurring accent.
- **Register colour-coding (this is the whole product idea — use it consistently):**
  **kirjakieli / written = purple `#6B46C1`**, **puhekieli / spoken = teal `#1F7C8E`**.
  Each register sits in its own soft rounded card tinted with its colour.
- Finnish nature accent palette (use sparingly for variety): lake `#1B4965`,
  forest `#2D4A2B`, sauna `#B5784A`, terracotta `#C25B3F`.
- Type: **Poppins** (semibold/bold) for all headings and display numbers; a clean
  humanist sans (Switzer or Inter) for body. Tight, slightly negative letter-spacing on
  big headings.
- Shapes: generous rounded corners (~24–32px), soft neutral shadows, calm and premium.
  No clip-art, no stock photos, no gradient-text clichés.

**Slide 1 — Title / hook**
- A large gradient **orb** as the hero.
- Wordmark: **PuhuScribe**
- Headline under it: **"Learn the Finnish people actually speak."**
- Small line: *A Finnish app for adults who need the language fast — and hit Duolingo's ceiling.*

**Slide 2 — The problem**
- Headline: **"Textbooks teach a Finnish nobody speaks."**
- Three short, stacked points (no paragraphs):
  - You arrived 0–18 months ago, working in English.
  - Residency, a job, a life — all waiting on your Finnish.
  - You tried Duolingo. You plateaued.

**Slide 3 — The wedge (the core idea)**
- Headline: **"Written and spoken Finnish — together, from day one."**
- Show ONE sentence in both registers as two stacked, colour-coded cards:
  - Purple card, label **KIRJAKIELI** (written): **"Minun nimeni on Maria."**
  - Teal card, label **PUHEKIELI** (spoken): **"Mun nimi on Maria."**
  - Quiet English gloss beneath both: *"My name is Maria."*
- One small caption: *Books teach the top line. The street speaks the bottom line. We teach both.*

**Slide 4 — How it works (and why you can trust the Finnish)**
- Headline: **"A method that sticks — and content you can trust."**
- Left: a simple 3-step flow (use small orb/dot markers):
  **Day One Sprint → Daily spaced repetition (real audio) → Shadowing.**
- Right: a small word card showing real pronunciation: **hyvä** with IPA **/ˈhyʋæ/** and a
  little speaker icon.
- Trust line at the bottom, set apart: *Every word is machine-verified as real Finnish.
  Real pronunciation, sourced — never invented.*

**Slide 5 — Try it now (the ask)**
- Headline: **"Try it now — be a tester."**
- Big and centred: the URL **puhuscribe-v2.vercel.app**
- A placeholder square for a **QR code** on one side `[QR]` (I will drop in a PNG).
- Three tiny steps beside it: **1. Scan or open the link  2. Sign up  3. Add to Home
  Screen** (iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen · Android: Install app).
- Closing line: **"Use it this week. Tell me what breaks."**

Keep total text low. Make slide 3's two register cards the visual centrepiece of the
whole deck. Deliver all 5 slides in the snow-white, orb-accented style above.

## Demo notes (for you, not on a slide)
- There is **no guest mode** — sign in normally during the demo. Have an account ready.
- **Day One Sprint resumes** where a learner left off (saved per user in the browser),
  so your demo won't restart from word 1.
- Show the **speaker tap** in Daily review so the room hears real Finnish audio.
- Good live demo beat: open the app right after slide 3 (the wedge), show one
  kirjakieli/puhekieli pair + tap audio, then land on slide 5 for the install.

## Real content bank (verbatim from the live app, if you want alternates)
Register pairs (kirjakieli → puhekieli → English):
- `Minun nimeni on Maria.` → `Mun nimi on Maria.` → *My name is Maria.*
- `Hei, kuinka voit?` → `Hei, miten menee?` → *Hi, how are you?*
- `Anteeksi, mikä sinun nimesi on?` → `Anteeks, mikä sun nimi on?` → *Excuse me, what is your name?*
- `Minä voin hyvin, kiitos.` → `Mä voin hyvin, kiitos.` → *I'm doing well, thanks.*
- `Näkemiin!` → `Moikka!` → *Goodbye!*

Real IPA (word → IPA): `hyvä` /ˈhyʋæ/ · `kiitos` /ˈkiːtos/ · `kahvi` /ˈkɑhʋi/ ·
`työ` /ˈtyø̯/ · `yö` /ˈyø̯/
