# Competitor Review Mining — Finnish Learning Apps

> **PROVENANCE NOTE — READ FIRST**
>
> This file is **WebSearch-sourced** (May 2026). Direct fetches to App Store, Google Play, and
> Trustpilot review pages are blocked in this environment (HTTP 403), and the search tool returns
> *synthesized summaries* rather than raw snippets. To protect against passing off paraphrase as
> real review text, the rule applied here is strict:
>
> - **VERBATIM (confirmed)** = text that appeared inside quotation marks in search results AND
>   survived a second exact-phrase re-confirmation search. These are marked **“quote”** with a source.
> - **Documented theme (paraphrased)** = real, recurring sentiment surfaced by review-aggregator and
>   professional-review sources, but *not* confirmable word-for-word. These are clearly labeled and
>   must NOT be quoted as if verbatim.
>
> **Star ratings and dates could not be independently verified** for most quotes because the review
> pages themselves were unreachable. Treat all attributions as best-effort.
>
> **Coverage honesty:** Duolingo, WordDive, and Mondly produced strong verbatim pools. **Drops and
> Memrise yielded confirmable praise quotes but NO verbatim user-review complaints** — their complaint
> sections below are documented themes only. To get publication-grade verbatim complaints with ratings
> and dates for those two, the review pages must be read directly (an authenticated fetch/scrape step).

## Marketing Copy Candidates

Ten **verbatim** phrases pulled from the confirmed quotes above, usable as landing-page headlines or
positioning lines. Each is labeled with its source app and *how* it could lead — either as an
**aspiration** to echo (what learners praise) or a **pain point** to position against (a gap a Finnish
product can promise to close). Every phrase below is verbatim-confirmed; verify against the original
page before publishing externally.

1. **"I'm actually learning Finnish! ... truly making you fluent in the language, not just memorizing."**
   — *WordDive, App Store.* Aspiration: lead with real fluency vs. rote memorization.

2. **"the first, and only, app I have found to learn Finnish through."**
   — *WordDive, App Store.* Aspiration: speaks to the scarcity of good Finnish tools — own that slot.

3. **"It's the only app that actually got me to practice every single day."**
   — *Duolingo, review-aggregator.* Aspiration: daily-habit promise, but for a tool that goes deeper.

4. **"All those hours ... had given me a huge vocabulary but left me more or less mute."**
   — *(from reddit-voice.md, The Dial — cross-file)* Pain point: the single sharpest anti-Duolingo line; lead with "stop being left mute."

5. **"Hearts have been replaced with 'energy' that plummets quickly, making it useless for learning."**
   — *Duolingo, Trustpilot.* Pain point: position against gamification that gates learning.

6. **"After 10 years of using Duolingo, it feels like a waste of time."**
   — *Duolingo, Trustpilot.* Pain point: the "years of streaks, still can't speak" frustration.

7. **"dumps way way way too much info at once ... you don't have time to absorb it all."**
   — *WordDive, App Store.* Pain point: promise paced, absorbable lessons.

8. **"I couldn't use WordDive unless I supplied a credit card number."**
   — *WordDive, justuseapp.* Pain point: lead with a genuinely free / no-card trial.

9. **"had to go to great lengths to cancel ... take you around in circles."**
   — *Mondly, Trustpilot.* Pain point: promise transparent, one-tap cancellation.

10. **"quick and fun lessons on a daily basis"**
    — *Mondly, Trustpilot.* Aspiration: the daily-lesson rhythm learners want — pair it with real YKI/B1 progress.

> **Note on #4:** that phrase originates in `reddit-voice.md` (The Dial, Dec 2025), not from an app
> store review. It is included because it is the most quotable anti-competitor line in the corpus, but
> it belongs to the learner-voice file, not this review file — labeled here so provenance stays clean.

### Methodology summary

- 4 parallel WebSearch passes (Duolingo+WordDive, Drops+Memrise, Mondly+supplementary, complaint backfill).
- Verbatim standard: quotation-marked in results **and** re-confirmed by exact-phrase search.
- All review pages (App Store, Google Play, Trustpilot, justuseapp, Memrise forums) returned HTTP 403 on direct fetch, so star ratings/dates are mostly unverified.
- Honest gaps: Drops and Memrise verbatim complaints could not be confirmed and are presented as documented themes only.
- Next step for higher fidelity: read the specific review pages directly (authenticated fetch/scrape) to capture raw 1★/2★ text with ratings and dates.
