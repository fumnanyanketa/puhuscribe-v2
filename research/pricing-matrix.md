## Competitor Price Cluster Analysis & PuhuScribe Recommendations

Pricing in this market arranges itself into three clear bands. The **low band (€4–€9/mo on annual commitment)** belongs to AI conversation apps and vocabulary tools that win on accessibility: Talkpal annual (~€4.29), Drops annual (~€5.01), WordDive annual (€5.27), Ling annual (~€5.72), and Talkio AI annual (~€7–€9). None of these offer YKI-specific content, and their low prices reflect that generic breadth. The **mid band (€10–€15/mo)** is where learners go for genuine depth: WordDive monthly (€10.99), Duolingo Super annual (€10.25), Talkio AI quarterly (~€11.16), Clozemaster Pro monthly (€14), LingQ monthly (€14.99), and Duolingo Max (€14.99). The **one-time exam-window band (€69–€130)** captures deadline-driven buyers who want a capped cost: YKI Finnish (€69 one-time), Ling lifetime (~€128), and Clozemaster lifetime (€327). Against this landscape, three candidate price points for PuhuScribe stand out. **(1) €7.90/mo on an annual plan (€94.80/yr):** this sits just above Talkio AI's annual rate and materially above Talkpal, signalling that YKI specificity commands a premium over generic AI conversation while remaining below Duolingo Super's annual rate — the most widely accepted paid price point in Finnish learning. **(2) €13.90/mo month-to-month:** this flanks Talkio AI's quarterly rate (€11.16) and Duolingo Max (€14.99), staking a claim to the "premium AI tutor with exam focus" slot that no existing product occupies; the €1.09 gap below Duolingo Max is deliberate — it keeps PuhuScribe cheaper than the market's best-known AI tier while offering YKI alignment that Duolingo cannot match. **(3) €99 one-time exam bundle:** above YKI Finnish's €69 but below Ling's €128 lifetime, this targets the permanent-residence cohort that faces a fixed exam date and prefers a single purchase to an ongoing subscription; a 6-month access window (rather than true lifetime) preserves upgrade opportunity if the learner misses their first test date. The recommended anchor is price point 1: the annual plan maximises retention and LTV, matches the cadence competitors use to lock in subscribers, and positions the monthly rate (point 2) as a natural short-term-commitment upsell for learners within 2–3 months of their YKI date.
