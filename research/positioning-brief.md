# PuhuScribe — Positioning Brief

*Synthesised from competitor-reviews.md, reddit-voice.md, pricing-matrix.md, and finland-apps.html. May 2026.*

---

## 1. The Wedge

**PuhuScribe is the only AI tutor that scores Finnish speaking and writing the way the YKI examiner does — in English, on your phone, before the B1 deadline that now controls your right to stay.**

AI tutors (Talkio, Talkpal, Duolingo Max) are generic: Finnish is one of 40+ languages, none know the OPH rubric, none simulate YKI task formats. YKI Trainer is closest but has a Finnish-only UI and no puhekieli training. YKI Finnish (€69) covers only speaking and writing. No product combines all four: AI feedback on all 4 subtests + English UI for immigrants + colloquial Finnish + explicit B1 path. That gap is PuhuScribe's opening.

## 3. Pricing Recommendation

**Anchor: €7.90/mo on annual plan (€94.80/yr).** Above the generic AI conversation floor (Talkpal annual ~€4.29; Talkio ~€7–€9) and below Duolingo Super (€10.25) — the market's most-accepted paid benchmark. Signals that YKI specificity is worth a premium.

**Flexible: €13.90/mo month-to-month.** Flanks Talkio quarterly (~€11.16) and Duolingo Max (€14.99), occupying the empty "premium AI tutor, exam-focused" slot. €1.09 below Max is deliberate.

**Exam bundle: €99 one-time, 6-month access.** For the B1 residency deadline cohort. Above YKI Finnish's €69, below Ling lifetime (~€128). Six months — not lifetime — preserves upgrade surface if the learner misses their first test date.

---

## 4. Three Competitor Weaknesses to Attack

**Duolingo:** Teaches tap-to-translate, not speech production. Grammar lessons removed in 2022. Teaches kirjakieli only. *"After 10 years of using Duolingo, it feels like a waste of time."* Attack line: *Ten years of streaks is not the same as speaking Finnish.*

**WordDive:** *"dumps way way way too much info at once — you don't have time to absorb it all."* Requires a credit card before the free trial begins. No speaking practice at any tier. Attack line: *Not a firehose. Not a credit card wall. Just Finnish.*

**Generic AI tutors (Talkio, Talkpal):** Finnish is language #38 of 40; no OPH rubric knowledge; conversation fluency ≠ exam readiness; neither simulates YKI formats. Attack line: *A generic AI conversation partner won't tell you if you'd pass. PuhuScribe will.*

---

## 5. Three User Priorities Before Launch

**1. YKI speaking simulator with OPH-rubric AI scoring.** The speaking subtest has a ~40% pass rate — the hardest subtest and the one every competitor skips. Without it, PuhuScribe is another conversation app.

**2. Puhekieli dialogue mode.** Every existing app teaches kirjakieli. Learners study for months and then *"don't understand a single thing"* when a real Finn replies. A colloquial Finnish track is the highest-leverage differentiator against the entire competitive set.

**3. B1 readiness score with a gap list.** Learners plateau at A1 with no "what next." A live score — *You are at A2.1. To pass YKI B1.1, close these gaps:…* — turns PuhuScribe from a practice tool into a residency preparation system and justifies the subscription over any one-time alternative.
