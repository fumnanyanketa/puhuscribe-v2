# Puhekieli Review

Review of all 500 sentence pairs. Each table lists the kirjakieli (standard written Finnish), the puhekieli (colloquial spoken Finnish), the transformation rules applied, and a flag where relevant.

Flag legend:
- Blank — straightforward, confident transformation
- REGIONAL — colloquial form varies by dialect
- UNCERTAIN — not fully confident the form is the most natural
- CHECK — multiple common colloquial realisations exist
